About the company

Premium glassware with purpose.

Vitreo Homeware Ltd. is a premium glassware manufacturer established in 2000, growing from a local artisan workshop into a regional brand focused on craftsmanship, personalization and sustainable production. The company creates custom-made home-use glassware designed to turn everyday products into meaningful lifestyle experiences. Its portfolio includes drinking glasses, wine glasses, tea cups, coffee mugs, bowls, jars, decorative collections, personalized gifts, corporate-branded glassware and limited-edition artisan pieces.

Vitreo serves affluent homeowners, gift buyers, hospitality businesses and corporate customers seeking premium customized products. Its production process combines digital design consultation, precision glass forming, laser engraving, quality assurance and sustainable packaging. The company also uses CAD software and digital rendering tools, while exploring future AI-powered personalization technologies. Sales are supported through e-commerce, retail partnerships, hospitality channels and export distributors.

Sustainability is central to Vitreo’s direction, with initiatives involving recycled materials, recyclable packaging, carbon-neutral operations and community engagement. The company reports annual production capacity of 1.2 million units, 96% customer satisfaction, a 68% repeat purchase rate and 82% recycled glass utilization.

Looking ahead, Vitreo plans to expand into Europe, North America and the Middle East while investing in automation, technology, premium product diversification and sustainability leadership. Its long-term ambition is to remain a scalable, innovative and responsible premium glassware enterprise that delivers distinctive products, dependable quality and memorable value for homes, hospitality partners, gift buyers and corporate clients worldwide. Through thoughtful design and responsible manufacturing, Vitreo aims to strengthen customer relationships, support creative customization and build lasting recognition as a trusted glassware brand.
